=== B. Why: the two results in CAT16's cone-like space (sRGB working basis, one reflection, normal incidence) ===
LMS of the result divided by the light's LMS (= the effective reflectance each cone sees); gain = the balance's scale (D = 1);
'S only' = dE after the balance when only S is taken from the RGB result (L, M from the spectral one); 'L,M only' = the reverse.
