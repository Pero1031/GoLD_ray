View transforms and the sRGB 'hiding' of the RGB-vs-spectral difference. Written by tools/studies/view_transform_hiding.py.
Views: Blender 4.5's config.ocio (Standard, Filmic, AgX, Khronos PBR Neutral) and OCIO's built-in ACES 1.3 CG config (SDR Video), applied with
OpenColorIO (tools/studies/ocio_apply.py). Input = the renderer's film, scene-linear Rec.709. Display values decoded with the sRGB curve and the
display's primaries; CIEDE2000 with the display's white (D65, Y = 1). 'Reinhard + clip' is the renderer's own PNG output (sections 3.5 and 3.7).
