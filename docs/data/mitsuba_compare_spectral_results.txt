Written by tools/test/mitsuba_compare.py. ratio = Mitsuba / GoLD_rayt (image mean, R G B) with its z; |z|>3 = share of blocks (expected about 0.3 %);
mean z = average block z (sign = which is brighter); |rel| = relative block difference (median, 95th percentile); noise = median relative noise of a block difference.
Spectral: Mitsuba's images are multiplied by 0.999001, which removes the offset of its spectral film (it normalizes by 1/106.7503
while its own y-bar integrates to 106.857, so every spectral render reads 1.001 times the luminance). Image means and blocks are compared in XYZ.
